## Description

The Fellowship Mandate, Accountability & Compensation Working Group will research and implement improvements to the Technical Fellowship's mandate, accountability, and compensation systems in response to present market conditions. The group will develop proposals for consideration by the Fellowship and DOT DAO, and will implement changes once they have received the required approval.

### Short-term work

- Propose and, when approved, implement a Fellowship remark stating that members drawing active salary may not have full-time employment, subject to removal from the Fellowship.
- Propose and, when approved, implement a Fellowship remark referring to or containing a Markdown document that assigns platform responsibilities to specific Fellows. Assigned Fellows may draw active salary if they are not in full-time employment, and would provide a short monthly report covering their subsystem's three-to-twelve-month goals, one-to-fourteen-day tasks, unexpected issues, and a rough breakdown of participating Members.
- Require Members drawing active salary to name, in their Argument for Retention, the Fellow supervising their work. Retention would require that Fellow to sponsor the Member by confirming that the Member directly contributes to developing or maintaining the subsystem and is among the best-placed people to do so.

### Medium-term work

- Research and, when approved, implement a mechanism for the DOT DAO to explicitly mandate Fellowship-supported subsystems, with Fellows able to propose subsystems for ratification.
- Research and, when approved, implement on-chain storage associating each subsystem with exactly one Fellowship Assignee.
- Allow Assignees to associate other Members with their subsystem as Delegates.
- Restrict active salary to Delegates and Assignees.
- Research and, when approved, implement a separate Fellowship Salary Treasury alongside the Fellowship Treasury. The Salary Treasury would track the number of payment periods its funds are intended to cover, allow DOT DAO funding to specify that period count, and make pro-rated payments across passive and active compensation when available funds are insufficient for a full payment period.
- Consider budget limits for each subsystem so that a maximum compensation amount can be stated in advance.

The group will define its impact measures as part of its initial research and update this proposal as its research and stakeholder feedback develop. The initial design is subject to revision as the group's work proceeds.
